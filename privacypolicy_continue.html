Privacy Policy

Last updated · 2026-07-29

Review this page once, then tap Continue to enter the lab.

Privacy information only. This page explains how GJGPZQ handles data on your device. Tap Continue when you are ready to open the main app.

All user data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

§01

Introduction

Welcome to GJGPZQ. We take your privacy seriously. GJGPZQ is a local engineering utility for differential amplifier gain analysis (Rf/Rin), common-mode rejection ratio (CMRR) estimation, hysteresis comparator threshold calculations (Vth+ and Vth-), Vin sweep previews, interactive circuit previews, and related reference material. Before using the app, please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By tapping Continue, you confirm that you have read this policy.

GJGPZQ provides on-device calculation and reference tools only. Every feature is available as soon as you open the main app.

§02

Information We Collect

GJGPZQ works entirely on your device. We do not collect personal information from you over the internet. The app may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Calculation inputs you enter in Workbench, such as feedback resistor (Rf), input resistor (Rin), common-mode voltage (Vcm), reference voltage (Vref), supply voltage (Vcc), hysteresis band values, Vin sweep ranges, and related numeric parameters;
  • Calculation results produced on your device, including differential gain, CMRR estimates, comparator upper and lower thresholds, saturation flags, and sweep curve data;
  • Saved run history records, notes, timestamps, preset identifiers, and associated parameter snapshots when you perform calculations;
  • App preferences stored locally, such as default Workbench mode, resistor unit display (kΩ/Ω), decimal precision, lab defaults, and intro topic order;
  • Files you explicitly export (JSON or CSV) through the system share sheet when you choose to save or send them.
§03

How We Use Information

We use information stored on your device solely to provide and improve GJGPZQ features, including:

  • Running differential amplifier and hysteresis comparator calculations in Workbench;
  • Displaying circuit previews, transfer curves, compare strips, and Vin sweep overlays;
  • Maintaining your History list, search filters, summary dashboard, and record notes;
  • Presenting formulas, examples, and reference material in Settings;
  • Applying your saved lab defaults, presets, and Workbench preferences;
  • Exporting history as JSON or CSV when you explicitly choose to export.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for purposes unrelated to the app without your consent.

§04

Storage and Protection

Your data is stored locally on your device using iOS storage mechanisms, including the app sandbox, NSUserDefaults for preferences, and local JSON files in the app's Application Support directory. Calculation history, parameters, and preferences remain on your device until you delete individual records in History, use Clear Inputs in Workbench, use Clear All Data in Settings, export and remove copies yourself, or uninstall GJGPZQ.

We take reasonable measures to help protect information on your device. However, no electronic storage method is completely secure. Please protect your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

§05

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not transmit your calculation inputs, results, or history to our servers. We may share information only when:

  • You have given explicit consent, such as when you export JSON or CSV files and choose where to save or send them through the iOS share sheet;
  • Disclosure is required by applicable law or a competent authority;
  • Disclosure is necessary to protect the legitimate rights and interests of you, us, or other users.
§06

Your Rights

You may:

  • Export all calculation history as JSON or CSV from Settings;
  • Import previously exported JSON history from Settings;
  • View, search, edit notes, and delete individual history records in the History tab;
  • Reset Workbench inputs with Clear Inputs without deleting history;
  • Clear all saved runs and reset parameters from Settings → Clear All Data;
  • Change lab defaults, resistor units, and decimal precision in Settings;
  • Uninstall the app to remove locally stored data from your device.

To ask questions or request assistance, please contact us as described in Section 9.

§07

Protection of Minors

GJGPZQ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a minor, please read this policy with your guardian and use the app only with their consent. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us.

§08

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Continued use of GJGPZQ after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
